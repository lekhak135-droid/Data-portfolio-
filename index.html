THE STORY BEHIND THE NUMBERS

The situation 2015 U.S. flight data had cancelled, diverted and incomplete records that could distort delay comparisons.The goal Prepare trustworthy airline, airport and seasonal performance comparisons.What I did Reconciled 5,819,079 source flights with Pandas and established eligible-flight rules for analysis.What I found Confirmed 89,884 cancellations and 15,187 diversions; final worst-airline and airport rankings remain unverified.
